# Roadmap

These are small collaboration opportunities, not delivery commitments:

- Document the supported usage-reading schemas with synthetic examples and
  clear field definitions.
- Add synthetic governor scenarios for boundary conditions and explain each
  expected advisory result.
- Document an optional host-witness workflow, including what evidence it can
  establish and what it cannot establish.

Dashboards and hosted services are deferred. No schedule or commitment is set
for them. This roadmap describes possible work; it does not claim that these
items are built, validated, or first of their kind.
