# Code of Conduct

Treat people with respect in project discussions and contributions. Keep
feedback about the work, make room for different experience levels, and
respond to disagreement without insults, harassment, threats, or disclosure of
another person's private information.

Project spaces are for constructive discussion. Content that targets or
harasses people may be removed, and participation may be limited when needed
to keep discussion usable.

For a security concern, use the private reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
instead of posting sensitive details publicly. Reports and other concerns are
reviewed as capacity allows; no response time is promised.
